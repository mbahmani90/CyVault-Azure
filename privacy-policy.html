CyVault Privacy Policy

CyVault, by Majid Bahmani Apps · Effective November 1, 2026

This policy explains what data the CyVault Android app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: cypress.newiot.co@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Account: your email address, display name, password (handled only by Microsoft's sign-in service, never seen by us) and an account ID. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with the sign-in service.
  • Balances: the purchases and income you enter: title, description, amount, currency and date.
  • Profile photo (optional): a photo you choose. It is resized and saved as a new image, which removes metadata such as location.
  • Server logs: technical request records used to keep the service running.

We don't collect your location, contacts or device identifiers, and the app has no ads or analytics.

Why we use it

Only to provide the app: to create and sign in to your account, and to store and show your balances and photo on any device you sign in on. We don't sell your data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Microsoft Azure in the EU (North Europe region). Microsoft acts as our service provider (Microsoft Entra External ID for sign-in, Azure Functions and Azure Storage for your data). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Each account can only reach its own data. Your profile photo is also cached on your device and removed when you sign out.

How long we keep it

Account data, balances and your photo are kept until you delete them or your account. Server logs are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can delete balances in the app at any time. To delete your account or other data, follow the steps on Delete your CyVault account. Under the GDPR you can also ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected, or object to its use, by emailing us. You can complain to your data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).

Children

CyVault is meant for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its effective date.
