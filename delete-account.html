Delete your CyVault account

CyVault, by Majid Bahmani Apps

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to cypress.newiot.co@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to CyVault.
  2. Use the subject Delete my CyVault account.
  3. We delete your account and data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name and sign-in methods (email and password, Google)
  • All your balances: titles, descriptions, amounts, currencies and dates
  • Your profile photo

What is kept

Nothing is kept on purpose. Technical server logs are removed automatically after 30 days.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Balances: in the app, long-press a balance, select the ones to remove (or select all) and tap delete. They are deleted from our servers right away.
  • Profile photo or anything else: email cypress.newiot.co@gmail.com from your sign-in address with the subject Delete my CyVault data and say what to delete. We delete it within 30 days and keep your account.
